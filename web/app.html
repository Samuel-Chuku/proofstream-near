    No wallet? No NEAR? You can still be paid.

    Post a public gist from your GitHub account naming the NEAR account to pay, then claim with your merged pull request. The relayer sends every step for you, and it can only send what the contract would accept. A new 64-character implicit account is created for you if you give one.
